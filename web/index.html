                      How it works

                      1. Cricsheet publishes every ball of every recorded match. The app downloads the IPL, T20 international and ODI files.
                      2. The training step replays each match in date order and records the situation after every over, using only what was known at that moment.
                      3. Several models learn from matches before 2024, are tuned on 2024, then sit an exam on everything since. The best-balanced one powers the Predict tab.
                      4. Venue par scores, team ratings and player records shown in the app come from the same data, so they refresh with every retrain.
